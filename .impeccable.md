## Design Context

### Users
Solo agency owner (web design/development) who uses this as their complete business + personal finance command center. Accessed throughout the day — morning planning, quick mobile checks mid-day, deep-dive sessions in the evening. Must be highly visible and actionable on mobile at all times, not just "responsive as an afterthought." The user never wants to think about forms, filings, or administrative overhead — the system should handle everything and surface only what matters.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: Quiet. Precise. Omniscient.

The system is the calm, precise person at the dinner table who knows everything — and can do everything. It doesn't show off. It doesn't need to prove itself. It simply has the answer, already computed, already formatted, ready to act on. Commanding confidence without ego.

### Emotional Goals
- **Commanding**: "I have total control over my business and finances"
- **Confident**: "Every number is accurate, every deadline is tracked"
- **Calm**: "Everything is handled — I can focus on the work that matters"

### Aesthetic Direction
**Tone**: Refined utilitarian — precision engineering meets warm restraint. Think: the dashboard of a high-end electric vehicle, or a Bloomberg terminal redesigned by Dieter Rams. Everything in its right place, nothing extra.

**References**: Linear (micro-interactions, progressive disclosure, keyboard-first), Stripe (typography, information hierarchy, the feeling that every pixel was considered). The user likes interfaces that feel *beautifully thought out* — where the right actions appear at the right time.

**Anti-references**:
- AI slop (purple gradients, glassmorphism, neon-on-dark, gradient text, side-stripe cards)
- Basecamp / Asana (too playful, too much personality that becomes grating)
- Any interface that would make someone say "AI made this"
- Purple as an accent color

**Theme**: Dark-first. Used at all times of day including mobile in daylight — needs strong contrast and readability in bright environments, not "cool dark mode" that washes out in sunlight.

### Color Direction
- **Accent**: Warm amber/gold — commanding (authority, wealth) yet calm (warm, earthy). Distinctive against the sea of blue/purple SaaS products.
- **Surfaces**: Dark, warm-tinted neutrals (not cold blue-grays)
- **Neutrals**: Tinted toward the brand hue (amber) for subconscious cohesion
- **Status colors**: Distinct but tonally warm

### Typography Direction
- Two-family pairing: distinctive display + clean body
- Monospace for all financial/numerical data (JetBrains Mono already loaded)
- Fixed rem scale (dashboard UI, not marketing)
- Clear hierarchy with strong contrast between steps

### Design Principles
1. **Show, don't decorate** — Every visual element earns its place by conveying information. No decorative flourishes that don't serve comprehension.
2. **Progressive disclosure** — Start with the answer, reveal the detail. The surface should be calm; complexity lives one interaction away.
3. **Mobile-native, not mobile-adapted** — The mobile experience is designed, not shrunk. Critical actions are thumb-reachable, not hidden in hamburger menus.
4. **Precision over personality** — Micro-interactions and transitions exist to convey state changes and spatial relationships, not to entertain.
5. **Right action, right time** — Contextual actions surface when relevant and disappear when not. The UI anticipates what the user needs next.
